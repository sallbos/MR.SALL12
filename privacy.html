🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2024

1. Introduction

MR.SALL AI ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our AI platform.

2. Information We Process

When you use our AI tools, the text you submit (prompts, documents, questions) is sent to our secure backend for processing. We do not permanently store this content after generating a response.

  • Chat messages are stored locally in your browser's localStorage only.
  • Uploaded PDF files are processed in memory and are not permanently stored on our servers.
  • We do not collect personal information such as your name, email, or account data.

3. API Keys and Security

AI API keys are stored securely as Cloudflare Worker environment secrets and are never exposed to the browser or frontend code. All API calls are made server-side through our secure Worker backend.

4. Local Storage

We use your browser's localStorage to save:

  • Your theme preference (dark/light).
  • Your language preference (English/Arabic/French).
  • Chat conversation history (stored only on your device).

You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's local storage.

5. Third-Party AI Services

Our platform uses third-party AI APIs (such as Google Gemini) to generate responses. Your inputs are transmitted to these services for processing. Please review their respective privacy policies.

6. No User Accounts

MR.SALL AI does not require registration or account creation. We do not collect or store personal identifiers.

7. Rate Limiting

To prevent abuse, we apply rate limiting based on IP addresses at the server level. IP addresses are not stored permanently.

8. Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies. We only use localStorage for preferences as described above.

9. Children's Privacy

MR.SALL AI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

11. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at: privacy@mrsall.ai
